# User Guide

## Start

Open **Ricci Surface Lab**. The main screen shows a 10 × 10 height-field grid with 100 sample points. Their heights are rendered as a colored, angled mesh for viewing on a phone.

## Run the simulation

Drag the **Discrete steps** slider to move directly between the initial surface (0) and the final displayed step (60). Press **Start simulation** to play the change automatically; the button becomes **Pause** while it runs. Press **Reset** to return to the initial surface.

Color indicates relative height only. It is not a physical measurement or calibrated curvature value.

## Interpret the result

The app is intended to build intuition for how a neighbor-based rule can smooth a sampled surface over time. It runs locally, sends no data to the internet, and does not connect to a real tabletop or motors.

> **Important:** Do not interpret the image as the result of a complete Ricci-flow computation or as evidence of a change in manifold topology. The model is deliberately simplified for learning.
